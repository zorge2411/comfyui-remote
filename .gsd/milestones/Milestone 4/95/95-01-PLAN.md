# Plan 95.1: Control Widgets and Socket Slots

## Objective

Stop `widgets_values` from drifting out of step with the node's inputs. Skip the control widgets the frontend adds after seed-like inputs, and don't let unlinked socket inputs take widget values (D-01, D-02). This is the main cause of the remaining C5 violations, e.g. `GeminiImage2Node.resolution = "fixed"`.

## Context

- .gsd/phases/95/95-CONTEXT.md
- app/src/main/java/com/example/comfyui_remote/domain/GraphToApiConverter.kt (`emitInput`, `findNextCompatibleWidget`, `WIDGET_KINDS`)
- app/src/test/java/com/example/comfyui_remote/domain/GraphToApiConverterWidgetMappingTest.kt (fixture style)
