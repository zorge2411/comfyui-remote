# Plan 101.1: Form Model and Seed Policy

## Objective

Build the pure domain pieces behind the decluttered form:
- a `FormLayout` that splits a workflow's inputs into prompt, negative prompt, main settings with human labels, and per-node groups;
- a `SeedPolicy` that respects a Fixed seed and reports the seeds it used.

Both are JVM-tested (101-CONTEXT D-01, D-02, D-03).
