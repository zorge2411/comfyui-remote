# Plan 102.2: Tabs, Connection Chip and Settings Tab

## Objective

Four labelled tabs (Workflows, Queue, Gallery, Settings), rooted at Workflows, with consistent Back behaviour. Connection becomes a pushed screen reached from a status chip and from Settings. History is removed (102-CONTEXT D-01, D-02, D-06).

## Context

- .gsd/phases/102/102-CONTEXT.md, .gsd/phases/100/100-UI-SPEC.md
- .gsd/phases/102/102-01-SUMMARY.md
- app/src/main/java/com/example/comfyui_remote/MainActivity.kt (the NavHost, `PaddedScreen`, the ConnectionScreen at around `:367-564`)
- app/src/main/java/com/example/comfyui_remote/ui/SettingsScreen.kt, HistoryScreen.kt, WorkflowListScreen.kt, GalleryScreen.kt, QueueScreen.kt
- app/src/main/java/com/example/comfyui_remote/ui/components/ConnectionStatus.kt, AppTopBar.kt, StatusBanner.kt
