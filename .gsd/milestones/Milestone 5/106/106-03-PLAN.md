      - key `"workflow-thumb-${workflow.id}"`;

      - the Workflows route passes `lastResults[workflow.id]`.
      - This gives the transition the same URL and Coil cache entry on both sides.
    - **Null scopes:** the modifiers do nothing (Gallery's pattern), and so do videos and workflows without a result.
