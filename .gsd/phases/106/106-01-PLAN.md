    - **API:**
      - `FormModel` gains `pinned: List<LabelledField>` (default `emptyList()`, so existing constructors still compile);
      - `build(inputs, positiveNodeId, negativeNodeId, pinned: Set<String> = emptySet())`.
    - **Order:** after the prompt and main roles are taken, the fields still left with a key in `pinned` go to `FormModel.pinned`, in input order, and out of their group. Groups left empty are dropped.
      - Pins on the prompt, negative or main fields are ignored (they stay where they are).
      - Unknown keys are ignored.
    - **Labels:** the humanized field name. If two pinned fields share a label, both become "<node title> · <label>".
    - **New tests:**
      - pinning a node-group field (e.g. a `ModelSamplingAuraFlow` shift, or a LoRA strength in the fixture) moves it to `pinned` and out of its group;
      - a pin on the seed key leaves it in `main`;
      - an unknown key changes nothing;
      - pinning a group's only field removes that group;
      - two pinned fields named `strength` from different nodes get node-prefixed labels.
    - Existing tests stay unchanged.
  </action>
